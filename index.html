Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about distribution timelines, pricing plans, and onboarding.

How long does music distribution take onto Spotify and Apple Music?

Standard release distribution typically takes 3 to 5 business days for major platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. For coordinated playlist pitching, we recommend submitting your song files at least 10–14 days prior to your official release date.

Do I keep 100% rights and royalties to my music?

Yes, absolutely. FameOrigin acts as your management, promotion, and distribution catalyst. You always retain complete master recording ownership and copyright. Royalties are accounted directly to your artist profile without hidden rights forfeiture.

How does Social Media Management work for artists?

Depending on whether you choose the Beginner, Working, or Rising plan, our marketing team designs, schedules, and writes copy for daily posts and stories, optimizes your bio link, manages audience interactions, and executes Google Knowledge profile SEO.

What are the benefits of the 360° Artist Management plans?

The 360° plans (Starter, Bright, and Flash) package distribution, social media management, personal profile websites, Google Panel verification, and custom promotions into one cohesive monthly retainer, saving you money while providing dedicated guidance.
